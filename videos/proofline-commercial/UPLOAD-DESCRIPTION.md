Proofline — Neural Storm FT-05: AI-Powered Automated Code Review

A small pricing change breaks an unchanged checkout caller. Proofline connects the change to its affected caller, shows source evidence, proposes a minimal repair, and runs the same protected test against the original, changed and patched snapshots.

This edited demonstration uses one actual, controlled end-to-end run. Animated diagrams explain that run; they do not represent an uninterrupted live review or establish production-wide accuracy. The source, model-generated patch and execution evidence are available in the repository.
